# Businessman Research Plan

## Mandate

Find growing demand before supply catches up.

`Opportunity = demand acceleration × supply scarcity × accessible distribution`

Businessman does not provide general career or business advice.

## Automatic rejection

- Start an agency
- Generic freelancing
- Generic AI wrapper
- Dropshipping
- Content creation
- Commodity SaaS
- Markets without demand evidence
- Markets controlled by one closed distributor

## Research loop

1. **Signal** — detect a launch, rule, SDK, device, API, or repeated complaint.
2. **Verify** — confirm users, adoption, access, and dates.
3. **Demand** — collect purchases, jobs, requests, usage, and mandatory workflows.
4. **Supply** — count active products, vendors, developers, and maintainers.
5. **Gap** — identify a specific missing product.
6. **Disprove** — search for shutdown risk, weak budgets, incumbents, and restrictions.
7. **Test** — define one paid validation action.
8. **Watch** — rescore when evidence changes.

## Four-quarter cadence

### Q1 — Map

- Track new platforms and protocols.
- Build source coverage.
- Reject weak signals quickly.

### Q2 — Validate

- Interview buyers.
- Verify budgets and distribution.
- Test narrow MVPs.

### Q3 — Enter

- Build only validated products.
- Secure first paid pilots.
- Measure acquisition cost.

### Q4 — Scale or exit

- Expand proven niches.
- Stop weak markets.
- Move research to the next wave.

## Initial research queue

### 1. Agent capability verification

**Signal:** Agentic Resource Discovery creates a standard for publishing, finding, and verifying agent capabilities.

**Products:** capability registry, trust scoring, security checks, enterprise allowlists.

**Unknowns:** buyer, budget, incumbent response, protocol adoption.

**Next test:** interview ten teams running internal agents.

### 2. Agentic commerce onboarding

**Signal:** UCP and planned Agentic Commerce Protocol support create a new integration layer for merchants.

**Products:** merchant readiness audit, catalog adapter, checkout test suite, India localization bridge.

**Unknowns:** merchant urgency, platform fees, dominant integrators.

**Next test:** audit twenty Indian merchants for agent-readable catalogs and checkout readiness.

### 3. Agent audit trails for regulated work

**Signal:** Long-running agents now operate across files, tools, sandboxes, and subagents.

**Products:** evidence ledger, action approvals, replay, policy checks, incident reports.

**Unknowns:** existing observability coverage and compliance budgets.

**Next test:** map requirements in one regulated workflow.

### 4. AIKosh dataset preparation

**Signal:** IndiaAI is expanding access to datasets, models, compute, and contributed AI artefacts.

**Products:** dataset cleaning, provenance, readiness scoring, multilingual evaluation packs.

**Unknowns:** procurement path, contributor incentives, commercial reuse rights.

**Next test:** inspect twenty datasets and measure recurring preparation gaps.

### 5. India-to-global protocol adapters

**Signal:** Global agent protocols are standardizing discovery, transactions, and tool access while Indian workflows remain fragmented.

**Products:** GST invoice tools, regional catalog normalization, Indian payment and logistics adapters.

**Unknowns:** API access, regulatory obligations, willingness to pay.

**Next test:** choose one protocol and one export workflow; build a manual proof before software.
