# Saved research

Saved runs are owner-scoped, immutable snapshots; the history index stores summary fields and loads result JSON on demand. Keep retention bounded (20 runs per owner).

The comparison panel fetches two runs only on request. It permits matching topic, geography, currency, and schema versions; findings match by stable opportunity ID. Unknown scores remain unknown; unmatched findings appear as added or no longer present. Do not interpret score change as a forecast.

Research tools such as AlphaSense support saved-query alerts and monitoring. A 2024 review of longitudinal clinical decision support found that systems often represent decisions as snapshots; use this as a design analogy, not business-market evidence. This feature compares saved evidence without claiming automated monitoring. [Study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38708184/) · [AlphaSense saved-search alerts](https://help.alpha-sense.com/hc/en-us/articles/41815267178899-Save-Searches-and-Create-Email-Alerts-in-AlphaSense).

For fast decisions, preserve the user's initial System 1 impression; use this comparison during System 2 evidence review. Neither impression nor score is a validated investment recommendation.
